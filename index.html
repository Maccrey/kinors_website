KINORA respects independent living. It helps the family notice meaningful changes without turning everyday life into surveillance.

KINORA does not provide medical diagnosis. It supports everyday change awareness and family confirmation.

Frequently asked

KINORA in a few questions.

Is KINORA a medical diagnosis service?

No. KINORA observes everyday changes and helps family members know when a human check-in may be useful.

Does the family see every conversation?

No. The guardian experience is designed around meaningful changes, evidence and recommended action rather than full conversation surveillance.

Which languages are supported?

Korean, English, Chinese, Japanese, Thai and Vietnamese. Unsupported browser languages fall back to English.
